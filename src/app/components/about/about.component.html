About Medgrain

Better Wheat. Better Flour. Better Everyday Meals.

Medgrain is a flour brand focused on quality wheat flour, consistent processing, hygienic manufacturing and reliable supply for everyday and business requirements.

We combine carefully selected wheat with modern processing practices to produce flour suitable for everyday household consumption as well as retail, wholesale and bulk requirements.
